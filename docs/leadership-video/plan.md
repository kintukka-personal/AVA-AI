# AVA leadership video: plan

Audience: WiseTech Global leadership. Format: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 25 s. Tone: `polished`, serious and restrained, with numbers that count up.

## Rubric

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is it? | AVA (Atlas Virtual Assistant), the governed AI front door for D365 Finance support, built in Copilot Studio. |
| Who is it for? | D365 users across Finance, plus the SMEs and BAU support teams who used to answer their "How do I…" questions. |
| What does it do for them? | Users ask in plain language in Teams and get the exact approved steps, cited to the Procedure Guide. Specialist questions go to FIN, MAP, TAM and WIA. |
| What sets it apart? | Governance: approved PGs and QRCs only, every answer cited, it never guesses, and it is read-only. New guides are picked up without retraining. |
| Most impressive claim | 3,113 conversations and 511 hours saved in 180 days, 94.6% of answers rated good, and 100% of pilot users would keep using it. |
| Visual hook | "7–15 min" counting up over a scatter of real Procedure Guide titles. |
| Real UI/flow to show | A Teams chat: "How do I create a purchase agreement in D365?" → AVA's real answer format ("According to the 34-Procurement (Purchase Agreements) PG", Topic, Action/Description table, link to the source). |
| Tone | Polished: navy and teal for structure, with AVA's crimson and plum hair colors as accents. |
| Share caption | AVA is now the first stop for D365 Finance support: 3,113 conversations, 511 hours saved, and every answer traced to an approved guide. |

## Angle

**From a 15-minute hunt to a cited answer.** AVA solved a navigation problem rather than a training problem. The video shows the problem, AVA solving it in Teams, the governance that makes it safe, and the results.

## Sources (every on-screen fact)

- 7–15 minutes: Gartner / IDC figure cited in the Ask Ava intro demo script.
- "How do I…" tickets: demo script and business case (support teams answering navigation questions).
- Answer format, guardrails, child agents: `config/ava/instructions.md`, `docs/architecture.md`.
- Purchase Agreements example: business case (34-Procurement (Purchase Agreements) PG, "Create Purchase Agreement").
- 3,113 conversations, 511 hours saved (Studio estimate), 94.6% good: `docs/analytics.md`, 180 days to 2026-10-23.
- 100% keep using, 4.6/5 usefulness, 60% fewer "How do I…" questions to support: pilot findings, `docs/history.md`.
- Live since May 2026 in Teams: `docs/history.md` (V2 launch 14 May, Teams 15 May).

Not shown, by choice: the credit-cost rise, the session drop in the last 90 days, and the licence limits. Those are tracked in `docs/open-items.md` and `docs/analytics.md`.
