HOW AVA WORKS:     Natural-Language Query Input

THE 4-STEP LOGIC   Users ask questions in natural language related to
                   D365 for intuitive interactions.

                   Retrieval from Approved Sources
                   AVA accesses approved procedure guides and
                   quick reference cards to find accurate answers.

                   Response Handling
                   AVA returns exact steps, asks clarifying questions,
                   or redirects safely if no source is found.

                   Cited User Guidance
                   Users receive guidance linked to cited sources
                   ensuring reliable and traceable information.
GOVERNANCE
AND
GUARDRAILS
SOURCE RESTRICTIONS, CITATION
REQUIREMENTS, AND SAFE REFUSAL LOGIC

Source Restriction
Only approved D365 PG/QRC sources are used to ensure accuracy and
reliability in information.

Citation Accuracy
Exact citations and page references are mandatory to maintain
transparency and traceability.

Clarifying Ambiguity
Ambiguous information is clarified via a built-in decision matrix rather
than guessed or inferred to avoid errors.

Safe Refusal Logic
Refusal is implemented safely when no approved source exists, avoiding
unauthorized actions.
