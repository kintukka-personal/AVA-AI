Create a clean, professional PowerPoint deck titled:

AVA – Atlas Virtual Assistant

Subtitle: Summary, Use Case, Benefits, and Pilot Findings

Audience:

Internal business stakeholders, leadership, and transformation/program teams.

Style:

Corporate, polished, modern, and minimal.

Use a WiseTech-aligned look and feel with deep navy, blue, purple, and teal accents.

Keep layouts clean with strong hierarchy, minimal clutter, and visually digestible slides.

Use icons, simple diagrams, metric cards, and clean charts where helpful.

Prioritise clarity over heavy text.

Tone:

Professional, concise, confident, and governance-focused.

Deck objective:

Summarise what AVA is, the problem it solves, how it works, the business benefits, pilot findings, and next steps.

Build the deck with the following slides:

1\. Title slide

\- Title: AVA – Atlas Virtual Assistant

\- Subtitle: Governed AI support for D365 documentation retrieval

\- Clean hero visual that suggests AI, guidance, and documentation support

2\. Executive summary

\- AVA is a governed AI assistant for retrieving approved D365 procedures

\- Built to reduce navigation friction and repeat “How do I…” support demand

\- Uses approved PGs and QRCs as the primary source of truth

\- Designed to be scalable, controlled, and auditable

3\. The problem

\- Users often knew the answer existed in documentation, but could not find the correct Procedure Guide, topic, or steps quickly enough

\- Manual searching through PGs was slow and repetitive

\- SMEs and support teams were absorbing too many low-value navigation queries

\- This was especially visible in post-training BAU and live system usage

4\. AVA use case

\- Training teaches the process

\- AVA supports users at the moment of need

\- AVA retrieves approved documentation, not undocumented advice

\- AVA is a navigation and retrieval layer, not a support replacement or workflow bypass

5\. How AVA works

Show a simple flow:

\- User asks a natural-language D365 question

\- AVA checks approved PGs and QRCs

\- AVA either:

\- returns exact documented steps

\- asks for clarification

\- safely redirects if no source exists

\- User receives cited guidance linked to the source

6\. Governance and guardrails

\- Approved PG/QRC source restriction

\- Exact citations and page references

\- No guessing, inference, or reconstructed steps

\- Ambiguity handled through clarification

\- Safe refusal when no approved source exists

\- No execution of transactions or bypass of approvals

7\. Benefits

Split into three columns or panels:

For users:

\- Faster access to approved steps

\- Less time spent searching

\- Easier navigation of D365 documentation

For support / SMEs:

\- Fewer repeat “How do I…” questions

\- Lower navigation-based support effort

\- More time for genuine exceptions

For the business:

\- More consistent process guidance

\- Better adherence to approved sources

\- Scalable AI-enabled support model

8\. Pilot findings

Use strong visual metric cards:

\- 100% would continue using AVA if available permanently

\- 80% used AVA 2–5 times per day

\- 60% saved 5–20 minutes per query

\- 4.2/5 confidence in AVA information

\- 4.6/5 usefulness rating

\- 60% agreed it reduced “How do I…” support questions

9\. What users valued most

Show a clean ranked chart or feature summary:

\- Clear step-by-step guidance

\- Faster access to approved procedures

\- Reduced need for support

Include short user quotes:

\- “AVA is a practical tool for retrieving information effectively and efficiently.”

\- “AVA will reduce the number of tickets that need to be raised.”

\- “It has great potential.”

10\. Learnings and next focus

\- AVA is strongest when questions map to approved PG content

\- Strict guardrails are valuable, but unsupported queries remain a challenge

\- User feedback improved clarification logic and resilience

\- Next opportunity is improving handling of non-PG / non-D365 queries without weakening governance

11\. Scalability example

Title: Auto-updating knowledge model

\- When a new Procedure Guide is added to the Atlas Training Hub, AVA can retrieve from it without manual retraining

\- Use the Purchase Agreements example:

\- New 34-Procurement (Purchase Agreements) PG added

\- AVA picked it up automatically

\- Successful retrieval validated for Create Purchase Agreement

\- Emphasise low-maintenance scalability

12\. Closing slide

\- AVA is a practical example of governed internal AI

\- It reduces documentation navigation friction at scale

\- It improves access to approved knowledge while preserving control and accountability

\- It has shown strong early value and is worth continued rollout and refinement

Design notes:

\- Keep each slide visually balanced and easy to scan

\- Avoid dense paragraphs

\- Use icons, callout cards, simple diagrams, and clean charts

\- Make pilot metrics a visual highlight

\- Make guardrails feel strong and intentional

\- Make the deck look suitable for both leadership review and internal showcase
