We’re excited to introduce **AVA V2**, upgraded using Copilot Studio and [now available in **Microsoft Teams**](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/app/?source=app-details-dialog&sharedAppResource=TaosSharedApp&titleId=T_7ce691b9-f3c6-ccc2-d713-05837b840ae8) — delivering a more powerful, connected, and user-friendly experience.

Following the successful pilot, the existing SharePoint-based AVA will be **updated to redirect users to the new Teams experience**, ensuring a smooth transition.

**What’s new in AVA V2: <span class="mark">\[SHORT VERSION\]</span>**

- Available directly in **Teams** with a persistent chat experience

- Improved memory for more efficient, continuous conversations

- Ability to **upload files** for more context-aware support

- Expanded capabilities beyond Q&A, including guided workflows

- More reliable and continuously improving experience

**What’s new in AVA V2 <span class="mark">\[LONGER VERSION\]</span>**

- **Available in Microsoft Teams  
  **Access AVA where you work, with a persistent chat that learns over time

- **Stronger memory & conversation continuity  
  **AVA keeps context across interactions, making conversations faster and more efficient

- **Upload files for smarter support  
  **Attach documents directly in Teams for more relevant, context-aware guidance

- **More powerful capabilities  
  **Beyond Q&A — AVA can guide workflows, support tasks, and provide structured assistance

- **Improved reliability & ongoing enhancements  
  **Built in Copilot Studio for a more consistent experience that continues to improve

**What you need to do**

**What this means for you**

AVA becomes your **central place for D365 Finance support** — helping you get answers faster, complete tasks more easily, and work more efficiently directly in Teams.
