# VERSION 1

\_\_Hi everyone,

We’re excited to announce the launch of **AVA (Atlas Virtual Assistant).**

AVA is your single, intelligent **D365 Finance assistant**—designed to guide and simplify every process from start to finish. AVA is now fully [available in Microsoft Teams.](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/app/?source=app-details-dialog&sharedAppResource=TaosSharedApp&titleId=T_7ce691b9-f3c6-ccc2-d713-05837b840ae8)

**About AVA**

- Access AVA directly in **Microsoft Teams** — where you work, with a comfortable chat experience

- Get **step-by-step guidance** for D365 tasks using approved procedure content

- **Upload files** to receive more relevant, context-aware support

- Designed to **guide processes and workflows**, not just answer questions

- Acts as your **central entry point for D365 Finance support**, powered by specialised AI agents

**What you need to do**

- Install AVA in Microsoft Teams: [CLICK HERE TO INSTALL AVA](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/app/?source=app-details-dialog&sharedAppResource=TaosSharedApp&titleId=T_7ce691b9-f3c6-ccc2-d713-05837b840ae8)

- Ask AVA any D365-related questions you have when needed

**What this means for you**

AVA becomes your **central place for Finance support** — helping you get answers faster, complete tasks more easily, and work more efficiently in Teams.
