Alright, let me start with a quick scenario.

Picture yourself as a D365 user. You’re doing your normal work, everything’s moving along… and then something weird happens. A posting fails, a screen looks wrong, or there’s a step you just don’t recognise.

And you pause.

You’re thinking, *“Did anyone ever show me this? Was this in training? Maybe…? I have no idea.”*

So you start the hunt. You poke through a few videos, skim through some guides, maybe try a couple of things.  
And minute by minute, you can feel your workflow… slowing… down.

Eventually, after seven, ten, fifteen minutes, you sigh, give up, and log a support ticket.

And honestly? That’s completely normal.

I did a little research on this yesterday and Gartner and IDC actually say the average ERP user spends **7–15 minutes** just trying to figure out what to do next when something unexpected happens.

So the big question for us becomes:

**How do we turn those 7–15 minutes into something closer to… 10-15 seconds?**

That’s where **Ava, the Atlas Virtual Assistant comes in.**

Ava is designed to take those “I’m stuck” moments and turn them into instant clarity.  
She doesn’t guess.  
She doesn’t make up steps.  
She doesn’t give general advice.

She pulls the exact, approved steps from the Atlas Training Hub, numbered, sequenced, cited, and hands them to the user instantly.

No more digging through PDFs.  
No browsing three different guides to find a solution.  
No more waiting for support.

You just *ask the question*… and Ava brings the answer.

**So let me quickly outline why this matters for us**

A huge chunk of BAU tickets are basically:  
**“How do I do this?”**

Jerry’s team reads the ticket, maybe goes to the same Procedure Guide the user couldn’t find, and sends the relevant steps back.  
It’s time‑consuming, repetitive, and honestly a bit frustrating for everyone involved.

This isn’t a training issue that can be addressed by providing refresher training  
This isn’t a documentation issue that needs to be addressed by more PGs.  
**It’s actually a simple navigation issue.**

And Ava solves exactly that.

**So before we jump in to look at Ava in action, lets take a quick look under the hood, at how Ava actually works**

A user simply launches Ava just asks a natural question, the same way they’d ask a colleague.

Ava searches the Procedure Guide index and returns:

- the exact steps

- with the exact reference

- and a link back to the source

And if the content doesn't exist?  
She doesn’t just invent an answer, she directs the user to BAU Support straight away.

This is a **document‑only retrieval solution**, with guardrails built in to mitigate hallucination and keep things trustworthy and relatively consistent.

For the BAU support team, this could be a big win:

- With fewer navigation tickets raised

- Better first‑call resolution rates

- And consistent instructions across the whole organisation

- Leaving Jerry’s team more time to handle real issues

And for users, it means…  
*No more 15‑minute confusion spirals.*

Just clarity. Immediately.

Alright that’s it from me, let’s jump into the demo.

Dan, over to you.
